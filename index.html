04Research

Academic Paper

Beyond the stage and the camera — a full research paper on how an animation studio builds lasting advantage through digital transformation.

IB Research · Business & Technology

The Innovation Strategy and Sustainable Competitive Advantage in Toei Animation's 3DCG Transformation

A case study of Toei Animation's hybrid 2D/3D production pipeline

Guiding question: how does Toei Animation's 3DCG transformation strategy facilitate building sustainable competitive advantage — and what most inhibits that facilitation?

Using a dynamic-capabilities lens, the paper traces Toei's move from hand-drawn 2D toward a 3D-assisted pipeline — 3DLO background tools, in-house Unreal Engine layout instruments, reusable style assets — and evaluates the strategy through SWOT, STEEPLE and Ansoff Matrix analysis. It argues that sustainable advantage depends not on the technology itself, but on dual artistic–technical leadership, reusable asset libraries, and capability-linked governance across a global production network.
